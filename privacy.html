Meridian

Privacy policy

Your log is yours.

No account, no ads, no analytics, no server. Meridian cannot see your log.

What Meridian keeps

Your fasts, meals and drinks, check-ins, weight, water, measurements and readings, and the name, height and goal you choose to enter. All of it is stored in a file on your iPhone or iPad. It is not sent to us or shared with anyone; Meridian has no server.

iCloud sync (optional)

If you turn on iCloud sync, Meridian keeps a copy of the log in the private database of your own iCloud account, so your iPhone and iPad show the same log. Apple stores it under your Apple Account; we cannot read it. Weigh-ins that came from Apple Health are not included. Turning sync off stops it; Erase this device, with sync on, erases the copy in iCloud too.

Apple Health (optional)

If you turn on Apple Health, Meridian saves the weight and water you log to Health, and reads weigh-ins from Health (for example from a smart scale) into your log. Health data is used only to show your own log. It is never used for advertising, never shared, and never stored in iCloud by Meridian. You can change access at any time in Settings → Health → Data Access.

Widgets, the Live Activity and Apple Watch

The widgets, the Live Activity and the Watch app read a small summary of the current fast that Meridian keeps on the device. The iPhone sends that summary to your paired Apple Watch directly, and the Watch sends Start and End back.

Reminders

If you turn reminders on, your device schedules them. Nothing is sent to a server.

Backups

Export backup writes a file only when you ask, and it goes wherever you send it from the share sheet. If you use iCloud Backup for your device, Apple may include Meridian's data in it, as with any app, under your Apple Account.

Purchases

Meridian Premium (monthly, yearly or lifetime) is bought through Apple. Meridian never sees your payment details; it only learns whether Premium is active.

Tracking

Meridian has no analytics and no advertising, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Deleting your data

Settings → Erase this device removes the log (and, with iCloud sync on, the copy in your iCloud). Deleting the app removes the copy on that device. Data already saved to Apple Health stays there until you delete it in the Health app.

Children

Meridian is not meant for people under 18, who should not fast without a clinician.

Changes and contact
